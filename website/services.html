Ways we can help

Support shaped around
what you need to do.

You do not need to arrive with a finished scope or a preferred method. Bring us the question, challenge, or decision, and we will help define the most useful approach.

01
Practical Research & Analysis

Find the evidence your decision needs.

We can explore ideas, verify claims, synthesize research, compare options, analyze data, or conduct rigorous original research. The depth depends on the stakes and how the answer will be used.

  • Rapid research and evidence scans
  • Policy, economic, and comparative analysis
  • Qualitative and quantitative research
  • Briefings and plain-language synthesis
02
Strategy & Planning

Turn priorities into a path people can follow.

We help teams clarify direction, involve the right people, evaluate tradeoffs, and translate goals into responsibilities, milestones, and practical next steps.

  • Strategic and organizational planning
  • Program and operational planning
  • Facilitation and stakeholder engagement
  • Implementation roadmaps and governance support
03
Evaluation & Learning

Understand what is working and what to improve.

We design evaluation around the decisions you face—not simply around reporting requirements. Findings are translated into concrete improvements and clearer resource choices.

  • Program, process, and impact evaluation
  • Monitoring and learning systems
  • Logic models and theories of change
  • Cost-benefit and cost-effectiveness analysis
04
Preparedness & Facilitation

Build plans that work under real conditions.

We combine standards, stakeholder knowledge, capability analysis, and clear technical writing to produce plans and exercises that teams can understand and use.

  • Emergency and continuity planning
  • Public-health preparedness
  • Gap, hazard, and capability assessments
  • Exercises, workshops, and after-action improvement
05
Models, Dashboards & Tools

Make recurring decisions easier.

When the question comes up repeatedly, we can build a model, dashboard, reporting workflow, or documented process that gives your team a maintainable way to answer it.

  • Tailored analytical and financial models
  • Dashboards, GIS, and data visualization
  • Automated reports and recurring analysis
  • Workflows, SOPs, and performance measures
